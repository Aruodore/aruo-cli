# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy, investigate proportionately, and may warn, restrict, or remove participants. Retaliation is prohibited.
