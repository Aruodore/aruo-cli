# Aruo product brain

`.design/` preserves the informal product thinking behind Aruo: evolving hypotheses, brand direction, principles in formation, long-horizon possibilities, raw ideas, competitor observations, and meeting notes.

It is committed for project continuity but **never shipped in Aruo-generated repositories, release archives, packages, template bundles, documentation sites, or user caches**. Packaging and blueprint conformance tests must deny `.design/**` explicitly.

## Boundary with formal records

- `.design/` captures thinking that may be incomplete or speculative.
- `research/` preserves evidence, methods, sources, and evaluations.
- `rfcs/` proposes substantial changes for review.
- `decisions/` records accepted consequential choices.
- root documents and `docs/` define public project contracts.

Nothing here overrides a public contract, accepted ADR, RFC decision, security policy, or governance rule. When an idea becomes consequential, promote it through research and the RFC/ADR process rather than treating this folder as hidden authority.

## Contents

- `product.md` — audience, problem, positioning, and product hypotheses.
- `vision.md` — long-horizon direction and boundaries under exploration.
- `brand.md` — identity, voice, naming, and visual intentions.
- `principles.md` — working product and design heuristics.
- `future.md` — possible future states and prerequisite gates.
- `ideas.md` — structured idea inbox.
- `competitors.md` — living market observations and watchlist.
- `meeting-notes/` — dated decision-support notes and follow-ups.

Do not store secrets, credentials, private user data, confidential partner information, vulnerability details, employment records, or unlicensed material here.
