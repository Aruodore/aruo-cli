# Brand direction

## Meaning

Aruo should feel like calm engineering competence: rigorous without bureaucracy, opinionated without arrogance, and ambitious without inflated claims.

## Voice

- Precise, direct, curious, practical, and generous.
- Explain constraints and tradeoffs rather than declaring universal superiority.
- Prefer concrete verbs and evidence over slogans.
- Credit upstream tools and contributors prominently.
- Never call a task “easy” or “obvious.”
- Never claim “fastest,” “secure,” or “production-ready” without scoped evidence.

## Product personality

The CLI should feel quiet, deliberate, and trustworthy. Progress is informative rather than theatrical. Errors are calm and actionable. Success output says what changed and what remains, not merely “done.”

## Naming

Use **Aruo** for the product and `aruo` for the command, package prefix, configuration vocabulary, and code identifiers where idiomatic. Define pronunciation and complete trademark/package/domain searches before the first public release. “Aruodore engineering ecosystem” describes the wider standards context; Aruo remains independently understandable.

## Visual direction

Favor excellent typography, restrained color, clear information hierarchy, readable diffs, and accessible status indicators. Color and icons reinforce meaning but never carry it alone. Avoid generic AI gradients, excessive mascots, fake terminal chrome, and ornamental dashboards.

## Brand evidence

The strongest brand expression is repository quality: fast startup, careful errors, tested examples, honest benchmarks, safe upgrades, excellent docs, and respectful maintenance.
