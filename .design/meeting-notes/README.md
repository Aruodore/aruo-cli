# Meeting notes

Store durable product and architecture meeting notes as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`. Notes should capture context, attendees, agenda, evidence reviewed, decisions proposed, unresolved questions, owners, due conditions, and links to resulting issues/RFCs/ADRs.

Meeting notes are not a substitute for formal decisions. Promote consequential outcomes into an ADR, RFC decision, roadmap, or public contract. Correct factual errors transparently; do not rewrite history to remove disagreement.

Do not record secrets, vulnerability details, private personal information, confidential partner data, raw transcripts, or performance judgments about individuals. Obtain consent before attributing sensitive viewpoints.
