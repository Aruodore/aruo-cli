# Future possibilities

This file explores directions; it does not make roadmap commitments.

## Repository intelligence

Build a local repository graph connecting packages, contracts, ownership, policies, decisions, tests, benchmarks, releases, and evidence. Prerequisite: stable observations and schema migrations with acceptable performance.

## Fleet policy simulation

Preview a policy or blueprint upgrade across many repositories and group conflicts before opening changes. Prerequisite: strong single-repository reconciliation, privacy boundaries, and organization governance.

## Architecture assistance

Propose documentation, tests, migration steps, or architectural alternatives grounded in repository facts, ADRs, and verified tool output. Prerequisite: deterministic validation and explicit separation of generated suggestions from accepted decisions.

## Onboarding paths

Generate role- and task-specific contributor journeys from actual project commands, examples, ownership, and architecture. Prerequisite: repository facts must be accurate and docs examples executable.

## Artifact ecosystem

Enable federated discovery of signed policy packs, blueprints, and plugins with transparent publisher identity and revocation. Prerequisite: mature permissions, provenance, incident response, and conformance infrastructure.

## Possible hosted layer

Offer opt-in collaboration, fleet history, policy administration, and artifact distribution while preserving open formats and local completeness. Prerequisite: a sustainable privacy, governance, export, and funding model.

## Things to keep deferred

Autonomous publishing, hidden remote execution, universal deployment abstraction, popularity-ranked trust, and aggregate “quality scores” without interpretable evidence.
