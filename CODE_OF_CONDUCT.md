# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free, accessible community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, nationality, appearance, or viewpoint. Technical disagreement is welcome; demeaning behavior is not.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with empathy, precision, and patience;
- critique ideas and evidence, not people;
- respect consent, privacy, boundaries, and differing experience levels;
- give credit and accept responsibility for mistakes;
- keep review proportional and explain blocking concerns;
- avoid exclusionary language, harassment, threats, unwanted attention, doxxing, and sexualized conduct.

## Scope

This applies in repositories, issues, reviews, discussions, events, chat, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the address published in the repository’s verified contact metadata before community launch. Until that address is configured, do not publish sensitive reports; contact the repository owner through a private GitHub security advisory and label it as a conduct escalation. Reports are acknowledged promptly, shared only with those needed to respond, and handled without retaliation.

Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participation based on impact, pattern, and safety. Anyone involved in a report recuses from adjudication. Appeals go to an uninvolved project steward. Aggregate anonymized enforcement information may be published when it improves accountability without harming reporters.

This project will adopt and attribute a recognized full community covenant before opening broad community channels; this Phase 0 policy records the required behavior and reporting design without inventing a nonfunctional contact.
